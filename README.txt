Turn your Suprema BioStar 2 access control system into a source of Homey Flows. When someone badges in, is refused at a door or presents a fingerprint nobody recognises, a Flow can switch on the hall lights, disarm a zone, send a notification or log the visit, with the person, reader and time available as tags. A Flow can also release a door. Events arrive the moment the server produces them, directly over your local network: no cloud service, no polling and no extra computer in between.

You need a BioStar 2 server running release 2.9.12.31 or newer that Homey can reach on your network, and a BioStar 2 account with Monitoring permission (plus Door control to open doors). This is an unofficial community app, not affiliated with or endorsed by Suprema Inc.
